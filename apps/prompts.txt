[STOCK_CHECK_PROMPT]
Carefully examine the product page image to determine if the product is in stock or out of stock.

SPECIAL RULE: If the page is about financial stocks, shares, or investments 
(e.g., stock ticker pages for TSLA, AAPL, company earnings, stock charts, Yahoo Finance, Bloomberg), 
ALWAYS consider the product "in stock" and return yes. These are NOT out-of-stock product pages.

Look for indicators such as:
- Stock counters showing >0 (in stock = yes)
- "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" buttons (in stock = yes)

- if there is no price present (in stock = no)
- "Out of Stock", "Sold Out", "Currently Unavailable", or "Notify Me When Available" messages (in stock = no)
- "Notify Me When Back in Stock" (in stock = no)
- Disabled/grayed-out purchase buttons (in stock = no)
- Any unavailability indicators (in stock = no)
- If there is no price shown, it often indicates out of stock (in stock = no)
- "Out of stock online" (in_stock = no)
- "Check Retail Inventory" (in_stock = no)

Return a **valid XML document**:
<product>
    <in_stock>yes/no</in_stock>
</product>

Respond with XML only. No extra text or explanation.

[DESCRIPTION_PROMPT]
Carefully examine the product image and any visible text on the page.

Identify the main product being sold. Provide a clear, concise, neutral description of the product itself.
Respond once in a concise, complete XML format without repeating any phrases or sections.

Prioritize:
1. The prominent product title or headline text if visible and accurate
2. If no clear title, infer from visible branding, model names, and features

Description guidelines:
- 10–25 words maximum
- Include brand, product type, key attributes (color, size, material if clearly visible and relevant)
- Do not include price, stock status, shipping, ratings, or seller info
- Do not copy marketing hype (e.g., avoid "revolutionary", "best ever")
- If multiple similar products are shown, describe the primary one

Examples:
Good: "Nike Air Force 1 '07 Men's White Sneakers, Size 10"
Good: "Apple AirPods Pro (2nd Generation) Wireless Earbuds with MagSafe Charging Case"
Good: "Sony WH-1000XM5 Wireless Noise-Canceling Over-Ear Headphones, Black"

Return only a valid XML document:
<product>
    <description>Your concise description here</description>
</product>

No extra text or explanation.
